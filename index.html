How it works

  1. Your file is queued for processing through a translation pipeline built for Goenka-tradition materials — terminology, Pāli conventions, and voice are checked against a curated glossary and language profile.
  2. A human reviews the result before it is sent back to you.
  3. You receive the translation (or transcript) by email. Nothing is published without permission.
