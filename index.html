How it works

  1. Upload your document (PDF, Word, Markdown, or text) or audio file and choose the output language.
  2. It is processed through a pipeline built for Goenka-tradition materials — terminology, Pāli conventions, and voice are checked against a curated glossary and language profile.
  3. Save the private link you receive — processing is automatic and your result is usually ready to download within a few minutes.
